23-Word BIP39 Dice Seed

Generate 23 seed words with 5 dice, read straight from the tables.

What you need

  • 5 identical dice (precision dice preferred, machined for even weight).
  • A shoe box. Its walls protect the dice from chipping and form an edge that fixes their order. No box? Stand a book open and let them line up in the V of the spine, or fold a card into a narrow trough.
  • The 6 printed table sheets that follow, and a pen.

Make each word (repeat 23 times)

  1. Roll: shake the dice in the box thoroughly, then tilt so they slide into one corner and line up. Read left to right: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.
  2. Re-roll check: if the 1st die is a 5 or 6 and the 2nd die is also a 5 or 6, re-roll all five. Go back to step 1.
  3. Re-roll check: if the 3rd die is a 5 or 6 and the 4th die is also a 5 or 6, re-roll all five. Go back to step 1.
  4. Find your page: it is determined by your 1st die, e.g. “1st die = 4”.
  5. Find your row: in the two left columns, go to your 2nd die (↓), then your 3rd die (↓).
  6. Find your column: your 4th die (→), across the top (1-6).
  7. Read the cell: it holds two words; your 5th die picks which (1-3 → top, 4-6 → bottom):
    1-3struggle
    4-6student
  8. Write the word on its next numbered line (1 to 23), in the order rolled. The order is part of your seed, so never sort or rearrange.

Worked example

Dice land: 1st=5   2nd=3   3rd=6   4th=2   5th=2
  • Re-roll check: 1st & 2nd = 5,3 ok · 3rd & 4th = 6,2 ok → keep. (a 5 & 6 pair would fail → re-roll)
  • Page: 1st die = 5.
  • Row: 2nd die 3, 3rd die 6.   Column: 4th die 2.
  • 5th die = 2 → top word → write struggle.

Two rules that protect your seed

  • Position only. Decide each die's role by where it sits (1st..5th), never by the number it shows.
  • Never fudge a re-roll. If you land on RE-ROLL, re-roll all five. Never re-roll a throw you have already accepted, and never move a die or swap faces, to dodge a word you dislike or a repeat. Repeated words are normal and expected; keep what the dice give. Re-rolling or rearranging after seeing the faces is what biases the result.

Finishing the seed

After 23 words, enter them into a hardware wallet or air-gapped signer (for example SeedSigner or Blockstream Jade) to get the 24th word. Never type them into an everyday phone or laptop. The result is a standard 24-word seed with 253 bits of dice entropy.

The 6 tables use the official BIP39 English wordlist (SHA-256 2f5eed…3b24dbda), read directly.
